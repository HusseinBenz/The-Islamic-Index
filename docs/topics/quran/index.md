# Quran Resources

Assalamu alaikum, and welcome. Here is a curated list of reliable websites where you can **read**, **listen to**, and **study** the Qur’an online.

## All in one

Theese websites include all essential features, allowing users to read the text, listen to audio, and view translations and transliterations:
- **[Quran.com](https://quran.com/)** 


## Read and Listen

- **[Quran.com](https://quran.com/)**
- **[Quran Online](https://quranonline.net/the-holy-quran/)**
- **[Alim](https://www.alim.org/quran/)**
- **[Islamic Finder – Quran](https://www.islamicfinder.org/quran/)**


## Read - Text Focused

- **[Quran.com](https://quran.com/)**
- **[Tanzil](https://tanzil.net/)**
- **[The Noble Quran](https://www.thenoblequran.com/)** 

## Listen - Audio Focused 

- **[Quran.com – Reciters](https://quran.com/en/reciters)** — Stream recitations by many renowned reciters with practical playback controls.
- **[Islamic Finder – Quran](https://www.islamicfinder.org/quran/)** — Listen to full surahs or individual verses with clear playback and translation support.


## Study

- **[Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com/)** — Detailed Qur’anic Arabic resource with morphology, grammar, syntax, and word-by-word analysis.


## Notes

- Most platforms let you switch between multiple **translations** and **reciters**, so feel free to try a few combinations until it feels right.
- For a smooth all-in-one starting point, **Quran.com** is often the easiest place to begin.
- For deeper cross-referencing across tafsir and hadith, **Alim** is a strong next step.
