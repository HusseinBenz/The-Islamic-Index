# Quran Resources

Assalamu alaikum, and welcome. Here is a curated list of reliable websites where you can **read**, **listen to**, and **study** the Qur’an online.


## Read and Listen to the Qur’an Online

- **[Quran.com](https://quran.com/)** — Modern, feature-rich platform with Arabic text, many translations, tafsir, word-by-word tools, and high-quality recitations.
- **[Quran Online](https://quranonline.net/)** — Simple, mobile-friendly experience for reading and listening without distractions.
- **[Alim](https://www.alim.org/)** — Comprehensive Islamic library with Qur’an translations, tafsir, hadith collections, and research-friendly navigation.
- **[The Noble Quran](https://www.thenoblequran.com/)** — A focused site for reading and studying the Qur’an with a clear, accessible layout.
- **[Islamic Finder – Quran](https://www.islamicfinder.org/quran/)** — Read and listen with translations and transliteration options, plus commonly used Islamic utilities.


## Read the Qur’an Online (Text-Focused)

- **[Quran.com](https://quran.com/)** — Clean reading modes with flexible display options (translations, script styles, and verse-by-verse navigation).
- **[Tanzil](https://tanzil.net/)** — A well-known Qur’an text project that is useful for clean reading, searching, and text-based reference.


## Listen to the Qur’an Online

- **[Quran.com – Reciters](https://quran.com/en/reciters)** — Stream recitations by many renowned reciters with practical playback controls.
- **[Islamic Finder – Quran](https://www.islamicfinder.org/quran/)** — Listen to full surahs or individual verses with clear playback and translation support.


## Study Tools (Arabic, Grammar, and Deeper Analysis)

- **[Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com/)** — Detailed Qur’anic Arabic resource with morphology, grammar, syntax, and word-by-word analysis.


## Notes

- Most platforms let you switch between multiple **translations** and **reciters**, so feel free to try a few combinations until it feels right.
- For a smooth all-in-one starting point, **Quran.com** is often the easiest place to begin.
- For deeper cross-referencing across tafsir and hadith, **Alim** is a strong next step.
